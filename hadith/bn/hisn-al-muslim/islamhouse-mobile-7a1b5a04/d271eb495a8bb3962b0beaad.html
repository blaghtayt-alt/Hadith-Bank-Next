“আমি আপনার কাছে ক্ষমাপ্রার্থী।”[২৫]

Editorial source-spelling note: The standalone prayer corresponds to the exact forgiveness formula in Aisha's Tirmidhi 7 account of leaving the lavatory. The Bengali book does not name a narrator in the local formula. Endnote 25 continues on physical p358 and carries its own attributed assessment.
