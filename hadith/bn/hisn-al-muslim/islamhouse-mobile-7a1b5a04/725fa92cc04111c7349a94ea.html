An excerpt of words or a report attributed to a Companion in the source.

Bengalibn
“তুমি পুরাতন করে ফেলবে, আর মহান আল্লাহ এর স্থলাভিষিক্ত করবেন”[২১]।

Editorial source-spelling note: The Bengali source prints this standalone wish immediately after the previous formula. The registered Abu Dawud 4020 records this wish through Abu Nadrah as a Companion practice, separate from the Prophetic supplication. Keep Bengali source note [২১] and its ৪/৪১ distinct.
