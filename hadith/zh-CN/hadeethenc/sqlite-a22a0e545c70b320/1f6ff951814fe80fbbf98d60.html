English · full narrationen

Related publisher version: Arabic wording or revision differs from the linked source version.

Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The Hour will not be established until people boast about mosques."

[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 449; Sunan al-Nasa'i no. 689; Sunan Ibn Majah no. 739]
