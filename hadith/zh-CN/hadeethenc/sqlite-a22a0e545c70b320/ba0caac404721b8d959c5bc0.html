English · full narrationen
Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) cursed the man who wears women's clothes and the woman who wears men's clothes.

[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 4098; Al-Sunan al-Kubra by al-Nasa'i no. 9209; Sunan Ibn Majah no. 1903; Musnad Ahmad no. 8309]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
艾布·胡莱勒（愿主喜悦之）传述： 安拉的使者（愿主福安之）诅咒那些穿着女性服饰的男子，以及穿着男性服饰的女子。

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）祈求安拉将每一个在女性专属服饰上模仿女性的男子逐出并远离其怜悯；无论是在样式、颜色、方式、穿戴方法、装饰，或其他方面的模仿。 或女子在男性专属的服饰上加以模仿；此举属于重大罪行之一。
