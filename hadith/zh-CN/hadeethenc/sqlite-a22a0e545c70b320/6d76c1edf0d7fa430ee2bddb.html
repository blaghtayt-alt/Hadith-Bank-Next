English · full narrationen
Abu Sa‘īd al-Khudri (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) say: "Whoever fasts a day in the way of Allah, Allah will keep his face away from the Fire seventy falls."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 2840; Sahih Muslim no. 1153]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
艾布·赛义德·赫德里（愿安拉喜悦他）传述：“我听到安拉的使者（愿主福安之）说： ‘谁为主道而封斋一天，安拉将使他的脸远离火狱七十个秋季。’”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）阐明，谁在为主道而奋斗时封斋一天，也有说包括在其他事务中，只要是为安拉而真诚举意、寻求主的回赐与报酬，那么安拉以他的恩典，使他与火狱相隔七十年之远。
