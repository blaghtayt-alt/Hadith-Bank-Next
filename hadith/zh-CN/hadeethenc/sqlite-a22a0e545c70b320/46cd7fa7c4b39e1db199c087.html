English · full narrationen
‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) heard a man preaching to his brother about modesty; he said: "Modesty is part of faith."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 24; Sahih Muslim no. 36]

据阿卜杜拉·本·欧迈尔（愿主喜悦他俩）传述，他说： “穆圣（愿主福安之）听见一人劝诫他的弟兄要知耻，便说:“廉耻是伊玛尼的一部分｡”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）听到有人建议他的兄弟不要太害羞时，明确告诉他，知耻是信仰的一部分，只会带来好处。 知耻是一种品德，鼓励人做善事，远离恶行。
