English · full narrationen

Related publisher version: Arabic wording or revision differs from the linked source version.

Ibn ‘Abbās (may Allah be pleased with him and his father) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said to Al-Ashajj, Ashajj ‘Abdul-Qays: "Indeed you possess two traits that Allah loves: prudence and deliberation."

[Sahih Muslim no. 17]
