English · full narrationen
Abu Mas‘ūd (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) said: "If a man spends something on his family, seeking reward from Allah for it, it will be recorded for him as charity."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 55; Sahih Muslim no. 1002]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据艾布·麦斯欧德（愿主喜悦之）传述自先知（愿主福安之）说： “如果一个人为其家人花费，然后以此寻求回赐，则对他来说花费即是施舍。”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之） 叙述道，如果一个人为自己应负有义务的家人花费，比如：妻子、父母和孩子等，然后他以此来接近安拉，并向安拉寻求回赐，那么他就有施舍的回赐。
