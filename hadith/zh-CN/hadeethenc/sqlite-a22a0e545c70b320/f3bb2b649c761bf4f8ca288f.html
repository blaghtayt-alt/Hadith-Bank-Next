English · full narrationen
Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "A man follows the religion (i.e. ways and manners) of his close friend. So, each of you should carefully consider whom he takes as his intimate friend."

[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 4833; Jami al-Tirmidhi no. 2378; Musnad Ahmad no. 8028]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
艾布·胡莱勒（愿主喜悦之）传述，先知（愿主福安之）说： “人随其密友的宗教，你们当中的每一个人都应当看看，自己与谁结交为友。”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）阐明，人会在品行和习惯上像自己的朋友和亲密同伴，友谊会影响人的品德、行为和处事方式，因此他指引人们要善于选择朋友；因为朋友会引导同伴走向信仰、正道与善行，并成为其伙伴的助力。
