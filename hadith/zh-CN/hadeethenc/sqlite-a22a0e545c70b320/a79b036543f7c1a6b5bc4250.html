English · full narrationen
‘Ā’ishah (may Allah be pleased with her) reported: I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) say: "No prayer should be offered in the presence of food or when he is resisting the need to relieve himself of the two filths."

[Sahih Muslim no. 560]

صحيح مسلم (1/ 393) (560).

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据阿伊莎（愿主福喜悦之）传述说：我听见安拉的使者（愿主福安之）说： “在饭菜已备好，或憋着大小便的情况下，不能礼拜。”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
安拉的使者禁止在吃食已备好的情况下礼拜，因为礼拜者内心向往吃饭，心系食物。 同样也禁止在憋着大小便的情况下礼拜，因为礼拜时他会忙于憋大小便。
