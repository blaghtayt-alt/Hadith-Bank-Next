صحيح البخاري (2/ 48) (1117).

English · full narrationen
‘Imrān ibn Husayn (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I was suffering from hemorrhoids (piles); so I asked the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) about the prayer and he said: "Pray while standing; if you cannot, then while sitting; and if you cannot, then while lying on your side."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 1117]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据伊姆兰·本·候绥尼（愿主福安之）说：我曾患了痔疮，然后我问先知（愿主福安之）该如何礼拜，他说： “你站着礼拜，如若不能就坐着礼拜，如若不能就躺着礼拜。”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）阐明，原则上说拜功是站着礼，除非在不能够站立的情况下，可以坐着礼。如果不能坐着礼拜，那么可以躺着礼。
