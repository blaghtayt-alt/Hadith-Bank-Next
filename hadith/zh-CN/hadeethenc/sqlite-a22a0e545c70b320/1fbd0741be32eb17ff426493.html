English · full narrationen
‘Amr ibn ‘Āmir related that Anas ibn Mālik reported: The Prophet (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) used to perform ablution upon every prayer. I asked: "What did you guys use to do?" He said: "Ablution would remain valid for any of us until he broke it."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 214]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据阿穆尔·本·阿米尔，据艾奈斯·本·马利克传述说： 先知（愿主福安之）曾每番礼拜都做小净，我说：你们曾是怎么做的？他说：只要没有坏小净，不必每次都洗。

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）曾每次主命礼拜都洗小净，即使小净没有坏也罢，那是为了获取报酬和优越。 只要小净尚未坏，那么一次小净可以多次礼拜。
