English · full narrationen
Jarīr ibn ‘Abdullāh (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Whoever does not show mercy to people, Allah Almighty does not show mercy to him."

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据杰里尔·本·阿卜杜拉(愿主喜悦他父子俩)传述，真主的使者(愿主福安之)说： “不怜悯别人者，不被真主怜悯｡”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）明确表明，不怜悯人的人，真主不会怜悯他。因此，仆人对被造物的仁慈是获得真主仁慈的最重要的原因之一。
