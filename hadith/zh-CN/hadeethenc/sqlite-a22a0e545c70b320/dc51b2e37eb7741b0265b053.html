English · full narrationen
‘Uqbah ibn ‘Āmir (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The worthiest stipulations to be fulfilled are those which make sexual intercourse lawful for you."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 2721; Sahih Muslim no. 1418]

Chinese (Simplified)zh-CN
据欧格柏·本·阿米尔（愿主喜悦之）传述说：安拉的使者（愿主福安之）说： “最应该完成的条件是，你们在缔结婚约时所设立的条件。”

[圣训翻译百科全书 — HadeethEnc · hadith number not supplied]

HadeethEnc.com · Translation origin: Human

Notes and comments · 1
先知（愿主福安之）阐明，最应该完成的条件是，与妻子合法结成时所设立的条件，即：妻子在缔结婚约时所要求的一些合理的条件。
