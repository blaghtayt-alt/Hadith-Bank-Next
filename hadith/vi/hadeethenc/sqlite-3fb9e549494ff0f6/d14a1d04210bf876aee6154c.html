{Bất cứ ai duy trì bốn Rak’ah trước hành lễ Salah Zhuhr và bốn Rak'ah sau đó, Allah sẽ cứu y khỏi Hỏa Ngục.}

A separately preserved source occurrence.

Arabic source textar
عَنْ أُمِّ حَبِيبَةَ رضي الله عنها زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَالت: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «مَنْ حَافَظَ عَلَى أَرْبَعِ رَكَعَاتٍ قَبْلَ الظُّهْرِ وَأَرْبَعٍ بَعْدَهَا حَرَّمَهُ اللَّهُ عَلَى النَّارِ».

English · full narrationen
’Umm Habībah, the Prophet's wife, (may Allah be pleased with her) reported: I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) say: "Whoever regularly observes four Rak‘ahs before Zhuhr and four after it, Allah will make him forbidden to Hellfire."

[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 1269; Jami al-Tirmidhi no. 428; Sunan al-Nasa'i no. 1816; Sunan Ibn Majah no. 1160; Musnad Ahmad no. 26772]
