English source excerpten

Reviewed source parallel; wording can differ between editions. Source excerpt; this parallel does not supply the complete source entry. The English follows the printed Arabic wording about being successors in the world. The Persian source additionally includes وارث خود; that additional expression is not reproduced by this English counterpart.

Abu Sa‘īd al-Khudri (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Life in this world is sweet and green, and indeed, Allah has made you successors there to see how you will behave. So, be cautious of this world and be cautious of women, for indeed, the first temptation of the children of Israel was due to women."

[Sahih Muslim no. 2742]
