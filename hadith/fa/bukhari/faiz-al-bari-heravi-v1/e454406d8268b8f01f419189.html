Hadith 29

Hadithفارسی — PersianTranslation: Human

Arabic source textar
عَنْ أَبي بَكْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: سَمِعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَقُولُ: «إِذَا التَقَى المُسْلِمَانِ بِسَيْفَيْهِمَا فَالقَاتِلُ وَالمَقْتُولُ فِي النَّارِ»، فَقُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ هَذَا القَاتِلُ فَمَا بَالُ المَقْتُولِ قَالَ: «إِنَّهُ كَانَ حَرِيصًا عَلَى قَتْلِ صَاحِبِهِ»

[Sahih al-Bukhari · الرقم غير متوفر]

English · full narrationen
Abu Bakrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) say: "When two Muslims meet to fight each other with their swords, both the killer and the killed are doomed to Hellfire." I said: "O Messenger of Allah, that is the case with the killer, but what about the killed one?" He said: "Indeed, he was keen to kill his companion."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 31; Sahih Muslim no. 2888]
