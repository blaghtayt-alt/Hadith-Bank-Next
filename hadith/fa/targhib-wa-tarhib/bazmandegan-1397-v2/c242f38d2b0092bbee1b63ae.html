English · full narrationen

Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.

Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Strive against the polytheists with your properties, souls, and tongues."

[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 2504; Sunan al-Nasa'i no. 3096; Musnad Ahmad no. 12246; Sahih Muslim no. 2490]
