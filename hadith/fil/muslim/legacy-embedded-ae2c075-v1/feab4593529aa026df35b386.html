Sapat na kabuhayan at nasisiyahang puso

Arabic · full narrationar

Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.

عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرِو بْنِ الْعَاصِ رضي الله عنهما أنَّ رَسولَ اللَّهِ صلَّى اللَّهُ عليْه وسلَّمَ، قال: «قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن أَسْلَمَ، وَرُزِقَ كَفَافًا، وَقَنَّعَهُ اللَّهُ بما آتَاهُ».

[صحيح مسلم، رقم 1054]

English · full narrationen

Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.

‘Abdullah ibn ‘Amr ibn al-‘Ās (may Allah be pleased with him and his father) reported that the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Successful is the one who embraces Islam, is provided with mere subsistence, and Allah makes him content with what He has given him."

[Sahih Muslim no. 1054]
