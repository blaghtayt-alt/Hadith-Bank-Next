سنن ابن ماجه (1/ 42) (61).

English · full narrationen
Jundub ibn ‘Abdullah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: We were with the Prophet (ﷺ) while we were young men close to maturity. We learned Imān (faith) before we learned the Qur’an; then we learned the Qur’an, and it increased us in faith.

[Sunan Ibn Majah no. 61]

Notes and comments · 1
Nagsabi si Jundub bin `Abdillāh (malugod si Allāh sa kanya) na nagsabi: Kami noon ay kasama ng Propeta (basbasan siya ni Allāh at pangalagaan) samantalang kami ay mga binatilyong nalapit sa pagkabinatang malalakas na matitindi.
