English · full narrationen
Abu ‘Ali Talq ibn ‘Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) narrated that the Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "If a man calls his wife to fulfill his desire, she should come to him even if she is busy with the oven."

Notes and comments · 1
Kapag pinaki-usapan ng lalaki ang asawa nito na makipag-talik,nararapat sa kanya na tumugon rito kahit na ito ay abala sa trabaho at walang ibang makakagawa nito liban sa kanya,katulad ng paggawa ng tinapay at pagluluto.
