HadeethEnc — Source entry 10641

Kasama namin ang Propeta-pagpalain siya ni Allah at panglagaan-sa paglalakbay sa gabing madilim,hindi namin alam kung saan ang Qiblah,kaya nagdasal ang bawat kalalakihan sa amin ayon sa pagharap niya,at nang kami ay kina-umagahan,binanggit namin ito sa Propeta-pagpalain siya ni Allah at pangalagaan-Ibinaba ang:{ Kahit saan mang dako na humarap kayo,ay nandoroon ang Mukha ni Allah}

عن عامر بن رَبِيعة رضي الله عنه قال: كنَّا مع النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم في سَفَر في لَيلة مُظْلِمَةٍ، فلم نَدْرِ أين القِبْلَة، فصلى كُلُّ رَجُلٍ مِنَّا على حِيَالِه، فلمَّا أَصْبَحْنَا ذَكرنا ذلك للنبي صلى الله عليه وسلم ، فنزل: ﴿فأينما تولوا فثم وجه الله﴾ [البقرة: 115].

English · full narrationen
‘Āmir ibn Rabī‘ah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: We were with the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) on a journey on a very dark night and we didn't know the direction of the Qiblah. So each man among us prayed in his own direction. In the morning, when we mentioned that to the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) the following verse was revealed: {So wherever you turn, there is the Face of Allah.} [Sūrat al-Baqarah: 115]
