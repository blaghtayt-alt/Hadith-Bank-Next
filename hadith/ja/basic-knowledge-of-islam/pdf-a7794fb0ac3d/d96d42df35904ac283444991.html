Research witness: contextual parallel. Wa-jalla thana'uk is present in the weak Prophetic insomnia report Tirmidhi3523 and a distinct Companion prayer in IbnAbiShayba. Nasb al-Raya discusses the addition to opening praise as absent from well-known reports. These phrase parallels do not establish the manual's funeral-only opening formula as a Prophetic instruction.
