ムスリムの砦 — Hisn al-Muslim — Source number 15 · Source entry 15

9．ウドゥーが終わった後のズィクル

Hadith excerpt日本語 — JapaneseTranslation: Human

A quotation preserved from the source; it may contain only part of the narration.

Japaneseja
「アッラーよ、あなたに賞賛と讃美あれ。私はあな た以外に真に崇拝すべきものはないと証言し、あな たにお赦しを請い、悔悟します。」

Research witness: variant. Abu Said explicitly attributes the post-wudu formula to the Prophet in Nasai al-Kubra9829, Tabarani al-Dua388 and Ibn al-Sunni30. Nasai immediately judges the elevation erroneous and the correct version mawquf (9830); Bayhaqi2499 likewise records competing mawquf paths. Classify as a disputed marfu report with source-authenticity HOLD, never as an independently established sound Prophetic instruction. The unrelated Ibn Umar catalog grading on one Dorar page does not grade these Abu Said branches. Evidence is limited to this separately identified witness; no whole-matn identity follows merely from formula containment.
