ムスリムの砦 — Hisn al-Muslim — Source number 113 · Source entry 113

30．睡眠時の恐怖や寂しさを感じた時のドアー

Hadith excerpt日本語 — JapaneseTranslation: Human

A quotation preserved from the source; it may contain only part of the narration.

Japaneseja
「私はアッラーの完璧な御言葉をもって、かれのお 怒り、懲罰、かれのしもべのもたらす悪、シャイタ ーンの囁き、そしてシャイターンが私のもとへやっ て来ることからのご加護を求めます。」

[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3893; Jami at-Tirmidhi · 3528]

Sunan Abi Dawud · 3893
Research witness: variant. Amr ibn Shuayb from father from grandfather transmits Prophet sleep-fright protection in AbuDawud3893 and Tirmidhi3528. The latter supplies punishment iqaab. Tirmidhi hasan-gharib and Darussalam Daif remain distinct attributed evaluations. Ibn Amr later writing/amulet practice is not promoted to Prophet instruction. Evidence is limited to this separately identified witness; no whole-matn identity follows merely from formula containment.
