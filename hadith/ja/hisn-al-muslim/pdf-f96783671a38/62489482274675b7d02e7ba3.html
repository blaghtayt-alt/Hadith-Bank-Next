Sahih Muslim · 3005
Research witness: variant. Suhayb transmits the Prophet narrating the boy-and-king story in Muslim3005, including this exact prayer twice from the persecuted boy. It is an excerpt of a Prophetic narration; the immediate supplicant is the boy, not Muhammad uttering a personal prayer. The compilation generalizes its use under fear-of-people. Evidence is limited to this separately identified witness; no whole-matn identity follows merely from formula containment.
