ムスリムの砦 — Hisn al-Muslim — Source number 131 · Source entry 131

38．敵に対するドアー

Hadith excerpt日本語 — JapaneseTranslation: Human

A quotation preserved from the source; it may contain only part of the narration.

Japaneseja
「アッラーよ、啓典を下されたお方よ、清算を敏速 になされるお方よ、敵軍を敗走させて下さい。アッ ラーよ、彼らを揺るがせ敗走させて下さい。」

[Sahih al-Bukhari · 2933; Sahih Muslim · 1742a]

ムスリムの砦 · Translation origin: Human

Sahih al-Bukhari · 2933
Research witness: variant. Abdullah ibn Abi Awfa reports the Prophet praying against the confederates in Bukhari2933, directly containing swift-reckoning and shake-them clauses. Muslim1742a is a distinct battlefield prayer with clouds and grant-victory wording; do not claim a single identical matn. Evidence is limited to this separately identified witness; no whole-matn identity follows merely from formula containment.
