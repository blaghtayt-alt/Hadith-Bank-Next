Саҳиҳ ал-Бухорий — Sahih al-Bukhari — Number in collection 43 · Source entry purpose.hadith.02

Оз бўлса ҳам давомли ибодат

Hadith excerptO‘zbekcha — UzbekTranslation: Unknown

A quotation preserved from the source; it may contain only part of the narration.

Translation origin is unknown; no human or AI origin is asserted.

Uzbekuz
Bas qil, toqating yetadigan narsani lozim tut. Allohga qasamki, ular malol ko‘rishmaguncha Alloh malol ko‘rmaydi.

[Sahih al-Bukhari · 43; Sahih Muslim · 785; Sahih al-Bukhari · number not supplied; Sahih Muslim · number not supplied; Musnad Ahmad · number not supplied]
