English · full narrationen

Related publisher version: Arabic wording or revision differs from the linked source version.

Anas ibn Mālik (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "By Allah, there is no true life except that of the Hereafter, so forgive the Ansār (Supporters) and the Muhājirah (Emigrants)."

[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 6413; Sahih Muslim no. 1805]
