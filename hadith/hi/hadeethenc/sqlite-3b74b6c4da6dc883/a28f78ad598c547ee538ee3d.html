HadeethEnc — Source entry 4244

मैंने अल्लाह के रसूल (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि व सल्लम) को देखा कि भूख के कारण सारा दिन पेट पर झुके रहते थे (ताकि भूख की कठोरता कम महसूस हो)। आपको रद्दी खुजूर भी नहीं मिलती था, जिससे अपना पेट भर पाते।

English · full narrationen

Related publisher version: Arabic wording or revision differs from the linked source version.

An-Nu‘mān ibn Bashīr (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: Are you not (living) in ease, with food and drink as much as you desire? I have seen your Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him), and he could not even find low-quality dates to fill his stomach.

[Sahih Muslim no. 2977]
