Sunan Abu Dawud · provider entry 3453

Original source entry

Review complete. A criticism of interpreting the phrase not one of us as not like us. It discusses the meaning of a quoted expression without supplying a separately bounded report. The complete original source entry and its references are preserved below.

العربية — Arabic · original source text

حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ الصَّبَّاحِ، عَنْ عَلِيٍّ، عَنْ يَحْيَى، قَالَ كَانَ سُفْيَانُ يَكْرَهُ هَذَا التَّفْسِيرَ لَيْسَ مِنَّا لَيْسَ مِثْلَنَا ‏.‏

English · original source text

Yahya said:Sufyan disapproved of the interpretation of the phrase "has nothing to do with us" as "not like us
