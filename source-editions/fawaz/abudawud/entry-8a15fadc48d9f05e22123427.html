Sunan Abu Dawud · provider entry 2840

Original source entry

Review complete. An explanation that removal of harm refers to shaving the head. This gloss does not contain the referenced report as a separate body. The complete original source entry and its references are preserved below.

العربية — Arabic · original source text

حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ خَلَفٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الأَعْلَى، حَدَّثَنَا هِشَامٌ، عَنِ الْحَسَنِ، أَنَّهُ كَانَ يَقُولُ إِمَاطَةُ الأَذَى حَلْقُ الرَّأْسِ ‏.‏
