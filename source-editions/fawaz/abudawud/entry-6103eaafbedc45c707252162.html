Sunan Abu Dawud · provider entry 4251

Original source entry

Review complete. A geographic identification of Salah near Khaybar. Keep the place-name explanation as supplied. The complete original source entry and its references are preserved below.

العربية — Arabic · original source text

حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ صَالِحٍ، عَنْ عَنْبَسَةَ، عَنْ يُونُسَ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، قَالَ وَسَلاَحُ قَرِيبٌ مِنْ خَيْبَرَ ‏.‏
